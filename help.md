# How do I get help in this course?



## Quantitative Reasoning Center 
You can book a tutoring appointment with a qualified QRA for our course.
Peer tutors for MATH121 are available on the following days and times:
  * Tuesdays from 7:30 PM-9:00 PM
  * Thursdays from 9:00 PM-10:30 PM
<!-- , and there are also some drop-in hours.  -->

Details are available at the [QR Center website](https://www.dickinson.edu/homepage/1785/quantitative_reasoning_center). 
Students can view the schedule and make appointments through [WCONLINE](https://dickinson.mywconline.com/).

## Instructor's office hours
Drop in any time during the instructor's office hours. No appointment is necessary. Alternatively, make an appointment for another time outside of office hours. Details are available at the instructor's [office hour web page](https://johnmaccormick.github.io/jmac-web/office-hours.html).
