# Exams

As stated on the course syllabus, the midterm and final exams are "closed book with note sheet." This webpage provides additional guidance on exams for this course.

* It will be possible to retake an exam under certain conditions.
<!-- See the separate [Exam retakes page](./retakes.md). -->

* All solutions must be written by hand on blank paper and turned in at the end of the exam.

* You may not consult any
printed, written, electronic, or other materials. Exceptions:
  1. you may bring into the exam _one page_ of handwritten notes (one side of US letter sized paper only) for Exams 1, 2, and 3; for the final exam, two sides of handwritten notes are permitted.
  2. You may also bring and consult the three pages of the MATH121 statistical tables.
  3. You may use a scientific, non-graphing, non-programmable calculator.

* Technically speaking, any material covered in any lecture, reading, or homework assignment is eligible to appear in the midterm or final exams. In practice, most exam questions will be similar to a homework question, an example done in class, or other assigned practice questions.

* In the three midterm exams, bathroom breaks are not permitted. The final exam will be divided into two parts with an optional bathroom break permitted between the two parts.

* Solutions must employ methods we have studied in class; other approaches will receive little credit.

## Exam 1

Exam 1 covers material from Topics 0-4 inclusive.

The duration of the exam is 75 minutes, and there will be questions
worth a total of approximately 75 points on the exam. So you should budget your time
at the rate of about one point per minute.  Try to allocate your time
wisely: spend only about 10 minutes on a 10-point question, and about
5 minutes on a 5-point question, and so on.

One side of handwritten notes are permitted. No bathroom breaks are permitted.

Likely questions and topics include:

* A true/false section. _Do not guess_, as incorrect answers are penalized: correct answer =+2 points, incorrect answer = -2 points, blank answer = 0 points.
* Hypothesis testing
* \\(p\\)-values
* Regression
* Empirical rule (68-95-99.7 rule)
* Describing a distribution in words
